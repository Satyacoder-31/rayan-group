OPERATIONAL SCOPE & CONTRACTING DOMAINS

DETAILED SCOPE OF WORK

Definitive contracting boundaries, technical disciplines, engineering standards, and turnkey deliverables executed by Heavy Civil Infrastructure.

SCOPE 01 AASHTO / UAE Municipal Codes

Highway Corridors & Arterial Road Paving

Full-depth highway construction, subgrade stabilization, aggregate road base, dense asphalt paving, and complete corridor delivery under strict municipal supervision.

Key Technical Deliverables & Activities:
  • ▸ Chemical and mechanical subgrade soil stabilization and California Bearing Ratio (CBR) verification
  • ▸ Dense bitumen macadam and high-durability asphalt concrete wearing course paving
  • ▸ Precast concrete curb laying, paved medians, safety barriers, and directional road signage
  • ▸ In-situ nuclear density testing, asphalt core sampling, and municipal compliance certification
SCOPE 02 Municipal Drainage & Water Authority Specs

Stormwater, Drainage & Municipal Utility Networks

Deep utility trenching, stormwater trunk mains, gravity sewer networks, potable water lines, and underground stormwater retention basins.

Key Technical Deliverables & Activities:
  • ▸ Deep stormwater trunk lines and precast reinforced concrete culvert installation
  • ▸ Ductile iron and high-density polyethylene (HDPE) water transmission mains with pressure logging
  • ▸ Underground attenuation tanks, soakaway borehole networks, and automated pumping stations
  • ▸ Live utility conflict relocation, deep dewatering schemes, and municipal connection handover
SCOPE 03 BS 7533 / Estidama Public Realm

Large-Scale Interlock Paving & Campus Hardscapes

High-durability interlocking concrete paving, pedestrian hardscaping, and vehicular plazas for educational campuses, commercial districts, and logistics terminals.

Key Technical Deliverables & Activities:
  • ▸ Over 35,000+ sqm of high-strength interlock paving executed at Al Qua School campus
  • ▸ Laser-guided aggregate bedding sand preparation, jointing sand stabilization, and mechanical compaction
  • ▸ Heavy vehicular traffic interlocking paving for bus turnaround terminals and logistics loading bays
  • ▸ Pedestrian walkways, tactile direction pavers, and ADA-compliant accessibility ramps
SCOPE 04 Earthworks Specs / 3D GPS Guidance

Bulk Earthmoving & Precision Site Grading

Millions of cubic meters of cut-and-fill earthmoving, rocky blasting and excavation, and precision structural platform preparation.

Key Technical Deliverables & Activities:
  • ▸ Mass cut-and-fill earthmoving with 3D GPS Trimble machine-guided excavators and motor graders
  • ▸ Rocky ground mechanical ripping, rock breaker crushing, and engineered structural backfill
  • ▸ 15T-20T vibratory roller compaction with continuous compaction value (CCV) digital logging
  • ▸ Plate load bearing capacity testing and topographical digital terrain modeling (DTM)
SCOPE 05 BS 5400 / Bridge Engineering Codes

Bridges, Culverts & Structural Containment Works

Reinforced concrete bridge substructures, precast prestressed deck girders, grade-separated approach ramps, and heavy retaining walls.

Key Technical Deliverables & Activities:
  • ▸ Reinforced concrete bridge piers, abutments, and elastomeric bearing pad installation
  • ▸ Precast prestressed concrete bridge I-girders and post-tensioned deck slab casting
  • ▸ Mechanically stabilized earth (MSE) retaining wall construction and elevated approach ramps
  • ▸ Bridge expansion joints, crash barrier parapets, and deck waterproofing membrane application

EQUIPMENT & TECHNOLOGY

Our civil infrastructure division operates Caterpillar, Komatsu, and Dynapac heavy earthmoving fleets equipped with 3D GPS automated machine guidance for millimeter-accurate grade control.

  • ✓ Earthmoving Fleets: Heavy hydraulic excavators up to 50T and high-capacity articulated dump trucks.
  • ✓ Grade Control: CAT 140M motor graders equipped with Trimble 3D GPS machine control.
  • ✓ Compaction Equipment: 15T-20T vibratory soil compactors with continuous compaction value (CCV) logging.
  • ✓ Paving Equipment: Asphalt pavers and mechanical interlock paving machines for accelerated delivery.
