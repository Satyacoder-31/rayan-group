CASE STUDY OVERVIEW

Al Lisaili Luxury Villa Development

Set in the serene landscape of Al Lisaili, Dubai, this palatial private villa blends contemporary architectural minimalism with classical Emirati hospitality traditions. Rayan Group is delivering turnkey engineering and contracting services, from deep foundation earthworks to structural concrete and architectural fit-outs.

The project features expansive open-span living areas made possible by post-tensioned beam systems, double-height curtain walls, private interior courtyards, and integrated smart desert landscaping with automated water recycling.

ENGINEERING & EXECUTION HIGHLIGHTS

  • Contemporary desert palace design spanning over 25,000 sq.ft of built-up area
  • Post-tensioned concrete structural frame engineered for expansive column-free living rooms
  • High-thermal-efficiency double insulated aerated blockwork suited for desert climate
  • Turnkey civil execution including boundary walls, majlis, pool, and staff pavilions
